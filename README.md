# 🚗 Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Evaluation

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on evaluating the Electric Vehicle (EV) market using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard explores key factors such as vehicle pricing, battery capacity, driving range, charging speed, customer ratings, safety ratings, and annual sales.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful visual insights to support market evaluation and comparison of electric vehicles across brands and countries.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyse electric vehicle pricing and performance.
* Compare battery capacity, driving range, and charging speed.
* Explore customer ratings and safety ratings.
* Evaluate annual EV sales across different markets.
* Identify trends and patterns to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI development

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard evaluates key metrics such as:

* Average Battery Capacity
* Average Charging Speed
* Average Driving Range
* Average Customer Rating
* Annual Sales Units
* Average Used Vehicle Price

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Interactive KPI cards for monitoring key EV metrics.
* Vehicle price comparisons across brands and variants.
* Analysis of battery capacity, driving range, and charging speed.
* Customer and safety rating comparisons.
* Country-wise market distribution and sales analysis.
* Interactive charts and filters for exploring vehicle performance and market trends.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to explore:

* Differences in vehicle pricing across brands and variants.
* Relationships between battery capacity and driving range.
* Variations in charging speed and vehicle performance.
* Customer ratings and safety ratings across vehicle categories.
* Differences in annual sales across countries.
